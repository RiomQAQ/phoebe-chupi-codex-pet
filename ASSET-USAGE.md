## Scope

This repository separates **code/documentation licensing** from **character artwork and reference terms** so that users and fellow creators know clearly what can be reused and how:

```text
Repository Licensing Breakdown
├── Code, Scripts, Tests & Markdown Guides ──► MIT License (LICENSE / LICENSE.zh-CN.md)
├── Project-Generated Sprite Artwork       ──► Non-Commercial Fan-Work Terms (below)
└── Third-Party Turnaround Reference       ──► Retained for Provenance Only (not relicensed)
```

The [MIT License](LICENSE) covers original Python scripts (`scripts/`), test suites (`tests/`), JSON layout/validation metadata, and documentation prose. Character artwork, reference images, sprite sheets (`pets/phoebe-chupi/spritesheet.webp`, `art/`), and animated previews (`previews/`) follow the terms below.

## Project-generated artwork

This project is an unofficial, AI-assisted fan creation based on **Phoebe** from Kuro Games' *Wuthering Waves* and the community's **Phoebe Chupi** chibi interpretation.

Within the scope of rights that the repository maintainer can grant:

- **Permitted uses**: You may freely download, install, study, modify, remix, and share the project-generated sprite sheets, frame crops, and previews for **personal, non-commercial use**.
- **Attribution requirements**: When redistributing or publishing modified versions, please:
  1. Retain this notice and link back to this repository (`https://github.com/RiomQAQ/phoebe-chupi-codex-pet`),
  2. Disclose that the artwork was generated with AI assistance (**GPT-6-Astra** and built-in image generation tools), and
  3. Clearly state any modifications you made.
- **Restrictions**: This permission does not transfer or supersede Kuro Games' intellectual property rights in *Wuthering Waves* or any other creator's rights. Commercial use, paid merchandise, and paid asset-pack redistribution are **not** authorized by this repository. Do not claim ownership of the underlying character or imply official endorsement by Kuro Games or OpenAI.

## Third-party references

The community three-view turnaround at `art/canonical/user-reference.png` and its three perspective crops (`reference-front-cutout.png`, `reference-left-profile-cutout.png`, and `reference-back-cutout.png`) retain their visible source markings. That turnaround is attributed to **蓝蓝铜离子**'s [Bilibili post (BV1zzAAzPEVA)](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1zzAAzPEVA/), which identifies the image as Doubao AI-generated. It is archived here solely to document visual provenance and proportion checks, and is **not** relicensed as original project artwork.

Historical comparison boards in `art/archive/` or `art/repair/` may include crops of that reference for side-by-side QA. Other third-party motion studies are linked directly at their original URLs in [Sources](docs/SOURCES.md) rather than redistributed in bulk.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding attribution or archived material, please [open an issue](https://github.com/RiomQAQ/phoebe-chupi-codex-pet/issues).

### 项目生成图片

本项目基于库洛游戏《鸣潮》角色「菲比」与社区二创「菲比啾比」形象制作，属于非官方的 AI 辅助同人作品。

在仓库维护者有权授予的范围内：

- **允许的使用方式**：你可以出于**个人、非商业目的**自由下载、安装、研究、修改和分享本项目生成的精灵整图、单格裁剪帧与动画预览。
- **署名与标注要求**：在分享或发布基于本项目修改的衍生版本时，请：
  1. 保留本说明并注明本仓库来源链接（`https://github.com/RiomQAQ/phoebe-chupi-codex-pet`）；
  2. 注明图像由 AI 辅助生成（**GPT-6-Astra** 与内置图像生成工具）；
  3. 明确标出你所做的修改。
- **限制条款**：本说明不转授或替代库洛游戏对《鸣潮》原作角色享有的权利，也不替代其他创作者的权利。本仓库**不授权**任何商业用途、付费周边制作或付费素材包转售。请勿宣称拥有原作角色版权，或暗示获得库洛游戏、OpenAI 的官方认可；如需商业或超出同人范围的授权，请自行向相应权利人取得许可。

### 第三方参考

位于 `art/canonical/user-reference.png` 的社区三视图以及三张去背视角裁剪图（`reference-front-cutout.png`、`reference-left-profile-cutout.png`、`reference-back-cutout.png`）均完整保留原始来源水印。该参考图署名为 **蓝蓝铜离子** 的 [Bilibili 投稿（BV1zzAAzPEVA）](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1zzAAzPEVA/)，原投稿注明由豆包 AI 生成。仓库收录该图仅用于交代造型来源与比例核对，**不作为本项目原创图片重新授权**。

`art/archive/` 与 `art/repair/` 中的部分历史对照板可能包含该参考图的局部截图以供比对；其余动作与表情参考视频均在 [参考来源](docs/SOURCES.zh-CN.md) 中通过原链接指向出处，不在仓库内分发原视频帧或批量下载的表情包。

如对署名方式或收录范围有任何疑问或建议，请随时 [通过 Issue 联系维护者](https://github.com/RiomQAQ/phoebe-chupi-codex-pet/issues)。
